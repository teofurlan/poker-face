# Poker Face

A party game where one person keeps a straight face while the rest of the room tries to make them crack. This glossary fixes the words the game, the code and the post use.

## People

**Player**:
The one person in the hot seat during a round, the only face the camera reads.
_Avoid_: subject, user, contestant

**Challenger**:
Anyone else in the room during a round, trying to make the Player crack.
_Avoid_: observer, spectator, opponent

**Hot seat**:
The role of being the Player for the current round.

## Play

**Session**:
One sitting of the game, from entering names to the final leaderboard.

**Lap**:
One pass in which every person in the session takes the hot seat once, in the order they joined.
_Avoid_: game, match

**Round**:
One Player's timed turn in the hot seat.
_Avoid_: turn, game

**Calibration**:
The quiet settling moment before a round starts, used to learn the Player's resting face.
_Avoid_: warm-up, setup

**Baseline**:
The Player's own resting face, learned during calibration, that every crack is measured against.

**Crack**:
One moment where the Player's face departs clearly from their baseline; a sustained laugh is a single crack.
_Avoid_: tell, lie, slip, fail
