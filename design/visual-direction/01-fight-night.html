01 · Fight Night

The hot seat is the ring and the room is the crowd. Poker Face borrows the chrome of a title-fight broadcast: a black arena under a single lamp, condensed slanted type, a scorebug that never leaves the top, a fight card in the lobby, a final card at the end. Everything is built to read from the sofa: one huge number per state, one colour that means "crack", and a clock you can see from the kitchen.

Tradeoff: instantly legible and loud, the strongest "party" energy of the three; also the most familiar broadcast vocabulary, and it leans on competition rather than comedy. The pressure meter is a segmented crowd-noise gauge, deliberately not a gauge with a danger zone.

One lamp slowly sweeping the black. A crack slams a red slab in with a clip-wipe, the counter thuds from red to white, a red frame bleeds out in a second. Nothing else moves.
Sound
Arena hush with a bell for each calibration second; a heavy thud plus camera-flash pop on a crack.
